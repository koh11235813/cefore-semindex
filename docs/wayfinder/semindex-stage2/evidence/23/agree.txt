CANDIDATE a's EPOCH IS NOT DISTINGUISHED BY ANYTHING IN THIS STEP, and that is said
here rather than left to a reader to divide two numbers.  A ratio is printed above
only where this run reads out the very rows the margin was taken on, because a ratio
of two numbers from two populations reads like evidence and is not; and where it is
printed, the standard error is the LARGER of the two, so the margin is a fraction of
the uncertainty on one epoch's own mean rather than a multiple of it.  The quantity
that would settle it is the standard error of the PAIRED per-row cross-entropy
differences between two epochs, which is smaller than the unpaired figure above and
is NOT computable any more: the keep rule deleted the two epochs that were not kept,
so no per-row cross-entropy of epoch 1 or epoch 2 exists on either machine.  So a's
kept epoch is reported as arbitrary among its three, and the number above is its
scale and not its test.
