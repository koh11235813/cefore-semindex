THE SEAM BETWEEN THE TUNING'S INPUT AND THIS RUN'S INPUT
The model was tuned on label rows read back out of a gzip-JSON file, and it is read
out below on the LIVE engine's own observation.  Those are two roads to one string,
and if they ever part the tuning and the scoring are of two different inputs while
both print plausible numbers.  decider.py's card-free self-check compares two CALL
SITES over one already-decoded row, which leaves the JSON round trip on both sides
of it and therefore unexamined.  So the comparison is made here, against the live
object: one labelling roll-in of a tuning seed is re-run (no model, no card), its
own captured observation of one decision is rendered, and that is held against the
rendering of the gzip row of the same (seed, k).
  the roll-in of seed 1 re-run in 0.0 s, 158 captured decisions, no model
  decision k 79: the roll-in arm the live run drew is 10.043 s and the row's is 10.043 s
  the live observation renders to 1157 characters, the gzip row of the same (seed, k)
  to 1157, and the two strings are equal, so the JSON round trip -- a tuple becoming
  a list, a non-string dict key becoming a string -- moves no character of the
  model input.  Shown failing on the row of k + 1 in part 5.

========================================================================
8  WHAT THIS RUN DOES NOT SHOW
========================================================================
- NOTHING about which candidate is better, and nothing about any decider's score.
  Seeds 1 and 2 are tuning seeds that both candidates were fitted on, so these
  are scores on training data.  Two seeds and two streams are four runs, which is
  not a sample of anything.
- Nothing about L on the testbed.  No run of this step touches the emulator.
- Nothing about the difference between L's score and an arm's being the decider's
  difference alone.  A decider whose send-time value differs from its
  resolution-time value skips resolve's identity early return and draws one extra
  tick lag from that pair's stream; the three fixed arms never differ and never draw
  it, F and L do.  So the comparator family consumes the pair streams differently
  from L by construction, in an unknown direction.
