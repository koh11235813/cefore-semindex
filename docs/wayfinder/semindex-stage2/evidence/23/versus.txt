
========================================================================
0  WHAT THIS IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT
========================================================================
THIS ANALYSIS IS NOT IN THE FROZEN PLAN.  spec.md fixes two statistics and one rule
and ana.py computes all three; this script was written AFTER the scores were seen.
IT SELECTS NOTHING.  The choice between candidate a and candidate b was made by
ana.py on the mean selecting score alone, and no number below can reopen it.
ITS INTERVALS ARE DESCRIPTIVE.  Nine comparisons are printed, no correction for
multiplicity is applied, and the pairs were chosen after the means were on the
screen, so an interval here says how far the 20 seeds let a difference move and NOT
that a difference was predicted or that one decider is better than another.

It reads one csv and nothing else: no card, no model, no run store, no label file,
no ROOT.  It prints no clock time and no path, so two runs of it are byte-identical.

========================================================================
2  THE PAIRED DIFFERENCES OF MEAN SCORE, SEED BY SEED
========================================================================
The mean score is `delay + weight x polls` over a decider's 800 runs, lower being
better.  A difference is of the FIRST decider minus the second, so a negative value
means the first scores lower.  The interval is the 95 % percentile interval of a
paired bootstrap over the 20 SEEDS: 2000 resamples from one integers(0, 20,
size=(2000, 20)) matrix drawn once with default_rng(20261122), i.e. predict.boot_index,
the same call ana.py makes, and the per-seed value is THE MEAN OF THAT SEED'S 40
RUN SCORES.

3  THE ANSWER WORDS, AND HOW FAR THE TIE RULE REACHED

logits winning, and an exact tie going to the first of (' short', ' mid', ' long'),
i.e. to the shortest interval.  The tie share is the share of a decider's own
decisions the tie rule settled, which is the reach of a read-out convention and not
a footnote: spec.md asks for it as a number for exactly that reason.

========================================================================
6  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
========================================================================
- No pass or fail on any decider, and no verdict the frozen plan does not carry.
  The plan's own verdict is ana.txt's, on the a-against-b difference alone.
- Nothing that corrects the selection bias.  The chosen candidate's figure is the
  minimum of two draws, so every row above that carries a or b inherits that bias,
  and the only clean correction is a set of seeds nobody has looked at.
- Nothing about a difference against an arm being the decider's difference alone.  F
  and L draw one extra tick lag whenever they change their mind between a send and a
  resolution and the three fixed arms never do, so the comparator family consumes
  the pair streams differently by construction, in an unknown direction.
- Nothing about the weight being right.  Its jackknife standard error is 1.685586 ms
  per poll and every score above moves with it; the band the plan tests against is
  built from exactly that uncertainty.
- Nothing about the tuning being reproducible.  Each candidate is one draw of a
  recipe whose two runs wrote different weights, so a difference between a and b
  carries one draw of tuning noise per candidate.
- Nothing about L on the testbed, and nothing about the agreement with the labels.
  No run of this step touches the emulator, and agree.py's numbers predict no score
  in either direction.

no check fired on real input; the failures in part 5 are the mutations
