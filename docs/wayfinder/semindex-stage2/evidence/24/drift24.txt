========================================================================
9  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
========================================================================
- NOTHING ABOUT GENERALISATION.  Seeds 1 to 20 are inside the 70 seeds candidate
  a's own fit already read; this is a within-fit sensitivity, not a held-out or
  selection-half measurement.  The held seeds (71-80) and the selection seeds
  (81-100) are untouched by this script -- nothing here is about them.
- NO RE-SELECTION.  Candidate a stays the chosen candidate (ana24.txt's VERDICT);
  this script does not compare candidate b and does not reopen that choice.
- ONE THRESHOLD.  The 0.5 cutoff on |within-run corr(decision time)| is the
  cross-verification's own after-the-fact choice, restated here and not
  re-tuned; no other threshold is tried.
- WHAT A SCORE CHANGE CANNOT SETTLE.  The 40 neutralised cells are held at their
  fit-row mean, which removes whatever STATE information they also carried, not
  only the clock-position information the within-run correlation flags.  A
  worse replay score after neutralising them does not by itself show that the
  shipped M was reading the clock rather than the state those same cells also
  describe; an EQUAL score would not have shown the opposite either, because
  the neutralised model could have picked up the lost state signal through the
  221 cells left untouched.  Section 7 reports, for each model, the decile
  shares of its own chosen interval and the within-run slope and correlation
  of that chosen interval against decision time; these numbers are printed as
  computed and are NOT characterised here as one model 'reading the clock'
  more or less than the other -- that reading is left to whoever compares the
  two printed slopes, not asserted by this script.
- THE EMULATOR-TO-REPLAY GAP.  Every number in this file comes from
  `eval24.one_run`'s replay port on logged-style observations; nothing here
  touches whether the replay port's own dynamics match the live Mininet
  emulator those logs were recorded from.
- NOTHING ABOUT WHY a difference, if any, has the sign it has beyond what is
  printed with its interval above; nothing further is inferred from it.
- Nothing about candidate b, p 0.4, the held or selection seeds, or any further
  neutralisation threshold.
