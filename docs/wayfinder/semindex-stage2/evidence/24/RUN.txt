evidence/24 -- what the s11a logs and ticket 09's labels can carry, before M is planned
2026-11-25.  Four scripts so far, in the order the later ones depend on the earlier:
features.py (the numeric form of a decision's input), charges.py (the cost of one
decision), precheck.py (what the logs can carry) and labelsig.py (whether the labels carry
learnable signal).  NOTHING HERE IS M, nothing here is a score, and no replay simulation
and no emulator run happened at any point.

WHAT THIS STEP ESTABLISHES
  1. A fixed-length numeric form of the one shared writing of a decision's input, proved
     to carry what the rendered text carries by inverting it and rendering the inverse.
  2. A per-decision cost whose sum over every period of a run is exactly the scorer's own
     cost, with the censored records' lower bound placed at a hop and nothing left over.
  3. That the full input vector explains far more of what follows a decision than the five
     scalar cells ADR 0004 line 5 permits, on seeds the fit never saw, and HOW MUCH of
     that is backlog and clock position rather than a verdict on an interval.
  4. That ticket 09's labels do carry learnable signal above their own class prior, that a
     closed-form linear fit on the same input reaches it, and that the five scalar cells
     carry label information whose own size is about a tenth of the full vector's and
     which never moves a three-arm argmax off 10.043 s -- so an argmax read-out of the
     cells equals the prior exactly, which is not the same statement as "no signal".
     Their Brier gain is a twentieth (candidate a) to a tenth (candidate b) of the full
     vector's.
  5. That the closed-form fits reproduce bit for bit in a second process, which is the
     check ticket 10's torch fit could not pass.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
  Nothing about M as a decider.  No policy is fitted, no arm is recommended, no replay
  score of anything is computed, and the per-decision agreement between the emulator and
  the replay stays failed in evidence/13.  No seed above 20 of the emulator logs and no
  label seed above 80 is read anywhere, so nothing here touches the selection half
  (81 to 100) or the measuring half (121 to 160).  The time-fair comparison of the three
  intervals is a property of the LOGGING POLICY's own continuation; the pooled rates do
  not identify a best arm and charges.py says why.
